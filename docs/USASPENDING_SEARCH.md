# USAspending prime contractor discovery

Official public API: https://api.usaspending.gov/docs/endpoints . Pinned upstream contract and filter documentation: https://github.com/fedspendingtransparency/usaspending-api/tree/24290f79dbec0e07238cd4e890a9e3ef6044d984 ; inspected api_contracts/contracts/v2/search/spending_by_award.md, api_contracts/search_filters.md and upstream CC0 licence. Uses documented schema/field concepts, not copied implementation.

Global trade catalog -> USAspending -> Research USAspending. Explicit owner consent for one keyless read-only POST search of the last 90 days' prime awards, six-digit NAICS, business recipient category and contract codes A–D. Fixed HTTPS origin and endpoint, up to three requests including one transient retry, 25 rows/page, 2 MiB/page, 10-second socket timeout, one-second spacing. No redirects, contacts, private profile transmission, submission, spending or live dispatch. Provider-side business/industry and signed-date filtering are reported assertions, not independently verified eligibility. Live schema and search semantics remain to be validated.

Preserve recipient name, optional UEI, agency, award ID, reported performance start/end dates, canonical award link, collection timestamp and page hashes. No award amounts, descriptions, contact details or arbitrary URLs in the projection. Performance end is not a response deadline; historical award is not evidence of current demand or available compensation. No lead marked research-candidate in tender sense, executable or zero-cash. Specialized To Do requires verified current legal entity, independent active subcontract demand, permissions, capability, terms and cash exposure. No government award-contingent commission assumption.

Unified Scout archive accepts the distinct award format, preserving bounded award metadata and regenerating specialized tasks. Existing four tender formats unchanged. Actual subaward search, recipient entity deduplication, current demand matching, independent evidence and live provider validation remain pending. No new dependencies, accounts or paid services.
