## Revenue route policy

- Direct digital service contracts, legitimate subcontract performance, private supplier research and procurement support are the strongest zero-cash candidates. Verify every opportunity's actual costs, skill, scope, client identity, lawful automation, acceptance and payment timing; zero cash does not mean zero labor or guaranteed payment.
- Affiliate programs and owned digital/IP licensing can avoid inventory. Confirm admission, channel, permitted automation, attribution, disclosures, intellectual property and royalties. FTC guidance: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- Government contracting and subcontracting need solicitation-level analysis, including FAR 52.203-5 (https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.203-5) and applicable FAR 52.219-14 (https://www.acquisition.gov/far/52.219-14). No contingent award procurement fee by default, no pass-through or unsupported representations.
- International services and trade sourcing require export/sanctions, tax and jurisdiction review; ITA CSL is an aid, not clearance. https://www.trade.gov/data
- Competitions and authorized bounties are speculative rewards, not accounts receivable. No autonomous security testing; specific scope and authorization required. Grants are restricted funding, never counted as earned revenue.
- Asset pricing, surplus sales and property research are intelligence only under the no-inventory model. Do not purchase or bid with Nexus. Business sale and real-estate introductions may themselves be regulated depending on conduct and jurisdiction.
- Licensed sectors are research-only hard holds: real estate/business opportunity brokerage (California BPC 10131: https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-bpc/division-4/part-1/chapter-3/article-1/section-10131/); freight (https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/registration/broker-registration, broker authority and $75,000 financial security); customs (https://www.help.cbp.gov/s/article/Article-1012?language=en_US); securities (https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/broker-dealers); consumer/commercial loan and mortgage brokerage (https://dfpi.ca.gov/regulated-industries/california-financing-law/); insurance (https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/105-type/findagtbrk.cfm). Exceptions and exact scope require qualified legal review. Do not presume a referral or fixed-fee label removes licensing obligations. No regulator clearance asserted.
- Additional research directions: professional-service directories and approved private RFQ exchanges, direct consent-based B2B demand, university research procurement, transit and municipal purchasing, authorized SaaS/agency partner programs, software/media distribution and lawful IP royalty licensing, reverse auctions and cooperatives. Validate each primary source, permitted access and contract rules before adding it to the catalog.
